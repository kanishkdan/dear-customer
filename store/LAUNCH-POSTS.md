## Booking and order update reply

Fair question. It flags promotional senders, not individual messages. If a number sends both offers and booking updates, blocking stops both. Keep businesses whose updates you need unticked or ignored. I can't promise selective filtering within one chat.

Optional follow-up if asked about opt-out without blocking:

Opt-out and STOP behavior also depends on the business. Some unsubscribe buttons stop all communication. If booking updates matter, leave that business unselected for now.

## Marketing budget reply

I can't promise it'll drain their marketing budget. It combines opt-outs, STOP, reports and blocking, plus a public record of repeat spammers. The goal is to make opting out easier and repeat abuse visible. Enforcement still sits with WhatsApp.
